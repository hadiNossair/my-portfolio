CHAPTER XIV: THE ASH FALLS
Aa 🔖
Archive Entry #42

The Mist
and The Shadow

Ash fell from the sky. Vin watched it, drifting down like dark snow. It blanketed the cobblestones of Luthadel, a soot-colored shroud that covered the misery of the skaa. She stood atop the iron gate of the Keep Venture, her cloak flapping in the wind.

The Lord Ruler had reigned for a thousand years. A thousand years of ash, a thousand years of servitude. But tonight, the mists felt different. They didn't just swirl; they pulsed. As if the heartbeat of the world itself was slowing.

"You're late," a voice whispered from the shadows. Kelsier stepped into the dim light of the lanterns. He looked as he always did—confident, scarred, and dangerously calm. "The Ministry is moving. We have three hours before the high-guard reaches the square."

Vin looked at her hands. She could feel the Steel burning in her stomach, a cold, metallic fire ready to be extinguished. "I'm ready," she said, her voice barely a whisper.

Ash fell from the sky. It always fell. But for the first time in a millennium, the people of Luthadel were looking up.
