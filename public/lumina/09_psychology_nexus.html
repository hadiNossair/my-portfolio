LIFE PHILOSOPHY

THE POWER OF WILL

"You only live once. Live it your way and do not let anyone ruin it. The strong survive, and loving life is the greatest win."

Weakness is not an option. Stand up, face your fears, and create your own reality.

"Before you think you are depressed or have low self-esteem, make sure you are not just surrounded by toxic people."

William Gibson / Sigmund Freud (Pop-Psychology)

Often, the problem is not in your mind. It is the toxic people around you draining your energy. Walking away from them is the first step to healing.

FACING THE DARKNESS

"Giving up is not the answer. True strength is looking into the darkness and fighting back. You are stronger than your pain."

The human mind is built to survive. Mental pain is a signal to change, not a signal to end. A strong person builds themselves from broken pieces.
