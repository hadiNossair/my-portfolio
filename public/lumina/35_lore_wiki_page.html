Knights Radiant

Organization

Overview

The Knights Radiant are an organization of Surgebinders on the planet Roshar who have formed a Nahel bond with spren. They were formed to fight in the Desolations against the Voidbringers.

They are divided into ten separate Orders, each of which has access to two of the ten Surges. All Orders share one Surge with the Order preceding it and the other with the Order following it, forming a circular chain of shared abilities.

The Ten Orders

Each order is dedicated to specific ideals and attracts individuals of certain temperaments. The first ideal, shared by all orders, is:

"Life before death, strength before weakness, journey before destination."

Recreance

Centuries ago, the original Knights Radiant abandoned their oaths and broke their bonds with their spren in an event known as the Recreance. Their Shardblades and Shardplate were left behind and became highly sought-after relics by modern lighteyes.
