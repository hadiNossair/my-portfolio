Harry Potter and the Cursed Child Act One, Scene One
Part One

King's Cross

The station is busy and crowded, full of people trying to go somewhere. Amongst the hustle and bustle, two large cages rattle on top of two laden trolleys. They're being pushed by two boys, James Potter and Albus Potter. Their mother, Ginny, follows close behind.

A thirty-seven-year-old Harry carries his daughter, Lily, upon his shoulders. The family navigates the thick crowds, pushing toward the solid barrier between platforms nine and ten. The weight of the Potter legacy is heavy, but here at King's Cross, it all begins again.

ALBUS: Dad. He keeps saying it.
HARRY: James, give it a rest.
JAMES: I only said he might be in Slytherin. And he might so...

Harry looks at his youngest son, seeing the familiar fear in Albus's eyes—the very same fear that had plagued him all those years ago before the Sorting Hat was placed upon his head. The fear of not belonging.

With a deep breath, they break into a run, hurtling toward the seemingly solid brick wall. The barrier swallows them whole, leaving the mundane muggle world behind as the majestic crimson steam engine of the Hogwarts Express comes into view, waiting patiently through the thick white steam.
