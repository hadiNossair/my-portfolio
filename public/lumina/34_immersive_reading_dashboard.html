Chapter 23: Honor is Dead

Kaladin looked down at the dueling arena. Adolin Kholin was outnumbered, his Shardplate cracked and leaking Stormlight. The crowd cheered, completely oblivious to the fact that they were watching a murder disguised as a sport.

Dalinar yelled at the surrounding highprinces. "Is there no one who will help him?" Dalinar demanded, his voice echoing in the stadium. But the other highprinces simply watched. It was political suicide to interfere.

Kaladin felt the wind whipping around him. Syl, his spren, danced in the air in front of his face. She didn't say a word, she didn't have to. Kaladin looked at Dalinar.

"Honor is dead," Kaladin said. "But I'll see what I can do."
