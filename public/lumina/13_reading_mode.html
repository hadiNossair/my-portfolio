The Arrival of the High Council

In the heart of the Shattered Plains, the wind carried the scent of approaching rain and static. It was a day like any other, until the rift opened. Elara stood upon the precipice, her robes billowing like a shadow against the stark grey stone. She had waited for this moment since the first signs appeared in the ancient scrolls.

The High Council had finally sent word. The Knights Radiant were to be summoned once more. Not as legends, but as the living shield between humanity and the encroaching void.

"Master Vance," a voice called from behind. It was a young apprentice, his face pale with exertion. "The Oracle has spoken. The ritual is complete. The gateways are stable."
