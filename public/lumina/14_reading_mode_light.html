ACT ONE

SCENE ONE: KING’S CROSS

A busy and crowded station. Full of people trying to go somewhere. Amongst the hustle and bustle, two large cages rattle on top of two laden trolleys. They’re being pushed by two boys, JAMES POTTER and ALBUS POTTER, their mother, GINNY, follows after. A thirty-seven-year-old HARRY has his daughter, LILY, on his shoulders.

The platform is thick with smoke, thick with the chatter of excited witches and wizards. You stand there, Albus, feeling the cold metal of your trolley handle pressing into your palms. The noise around you is deafening, but all you can hear is the frantic, terrified beating of your own heart. Your brother James has already run ahead, disappearing into the solid brick barrier between platforms nine and ten.

You look up at your father. The great Harry Potter. The man everyone whispers about as he passes. He smiles down at you, but you wonder if he can see the fear behind your eyes. What if you're not like him? What if the sorting hat places you... somewhere else?

ALBUS

"Dad. He keeps saying it."

HARRY

"James, give it a rest."

JAMES

"I only said he might be in Slytherin. And he might so..." (He catches his dad’s eye) "Fine."

You watch James run off, arrogant and confident. You grip the trolley tighter. The word "Slytherin" hangs in the air like a curse. You look at your father again, desperation creeping into your voice.
ALBUS

"You’ll write to me, won’t you?"

HARRY

"Every day if you want us to."

ALBUS

"No. Not every day. James says most people only get letters from home about once a month."

Harry crouches down to your level. For a moment, the bustling station fades away. It's just you and him. The Boy Who Lived, and the boy who wishes he was anyone else.
HARRY

"Albus Severus, you were named for two headmasters of Hogwarts. One of them was a Slytherin and he was probably the bravest man I ever knew."

His words are supposed to comfort you, but as you stare at the barrier, the green mist of anxiety in your mind only thickens. You take a deep breath. It's time. You run toward the barrier, closing your eyes, waiting for the crash. Instead, you emerge into a world of steam, magic, and a legacy that threatens to swallow you whole.

~ End of Scene One ~
